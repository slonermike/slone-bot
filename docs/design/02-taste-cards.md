# 02 Taste Cards (read-only; maintained by Slone)

Each card strips a game he loves to its heart: the loop and the feeling, not the theme or the surface mechanics. Use these to understand what to aim for, never to clone.

## Format
**Heart:** one line. **Loop:** the repeating cycle. **Feeling:** what it does to the player. **Atoms:** concept-library tags.

## Loves

### Tony Hawk
**Heart:** quick runs, open-ended traversal mechanics, repeat until you perfect the line. It is not about skateboarding.
**Loop:** short timed run, chain verbs into a combo, chase a better line, restart instantly.
**Feeling:** flow, mastery by self-chosen repetition (not humiliation), "one more run".
**Atoms:** short-run loop, combo chain, traversal toy, instant restart, self-set goals.

### The Legend of Zelda (all, especially Ocarina of Time; not Tears of the Kingdom)
**Heart:** a dense, learnable world where every new tool recolors places you have already been.
**Loop:** find an item, gain a verb, revisit the world with new eyes, find the next thing.
**Feeling:** curiosity, competence growing steadily, readable combat (targeting).
**Atoms:** item-as-new-verb, revisit-with-power, dense small world, readable lock-on combat.

### RollerCoaster Tycoon
**Heart:** build a system, then watch tiny people react to it. Tweak, observe, ride your creation.
**Loop:** place, observe consequences, adjust, grow.
**Feeling:** quiet pride, tinkering, delightful small stories from simulated people.
**Atoms:** build-and-watch, simulated crowd, readable system, consequence accumulation.

### Tetris
**Heart:** constant pressure with always-readable choices; perfect fits feel like relief.
**Loop:** piece arrives, decide, place, clear, speed ramps until you break.
**Feeling:** tension and release, clean decisions, flow.
**Atoms:** escalating pressure, readable choices, tension-release, speed ramp.

### Red Dead Redemption
**Heart:** weighty movement through a reactive world where travel itself produces unplanned encounters.
**Loop:** ride, stumble into something, handle it, ride on.
**Feeling:** immersion, unplanned stories, being a person in a place.
**Atoms:** weighty traversal, emergent encounters, reactive world.

### Mario
**Heart:** the movement is the toy; it feels good before any level exists; level design teaches silently.
**Loop:** move, discover what movement can do, apply it to a new layout.
**Feeling:** playfulness, quiet teaching, joy in motion.
**Atoms:** toy-like core verb, silent teaching, movement mastery by exploration.

### Metroid
**Heart:** isolation, plus ability gating that makes you revisit old spaces more powerful.
**Loop:** explore, hit a wall, find a power, return, open the wall.
**Feeling:** solitude, discovery, growing power.
**Atoms:** ability gating, revisit-with-power, isolation atmosphere.

### Timberborn
**Heart:** logistics against a looming constraint (drought, tides); you live inside your layout's consequences.
**Loop:** plan, build, survive the pressure cycle, learn from the damage, redesign.
**Feeling:** tense stewardship, satisfying engineering, back pressure that gives open-ended play stakes.
**Atoms:** looming cyclical pressure, logistics layout, consequence of design, recoverable setback.

### The Sims
**Heart:** a household with a history; steady growth, successes, and recovering from failures.
**Loop:** tend needs, make choices, watch outcomes accumulate, recover from setbacks.
**Feeling:** long-term attachment, narrative accumulation, comfort in recovery.
**Atoms:** household with a history, recoverable setback loop, narrative accumulation.

### GTA / Saints Row (minus the grit)
**Heart:** a toy-box city where driving is the base verb. Heat and chases create pressure, and constant discovery of new vehicles, weapons, and play patterns keeps exploration paying out.
**Loop:** roam, find a new toy, cause a little chaos, escape the heat, repeat.
**Feeling:** thrilling escape, curiosity about what else is out there, playful freedom.
**Atoms:** escalating pursuit, toy-like traversal (driving), new-verb discovery, open sandbox with back pressure.

### Cloud Gardens
**Heart:** bring dead places back to life; the payoff is watching beauty take form from something ruined. The mechanic can be awkward; the transformation is the point.
**Loop:** place a seed or object, tend it, watch the scene grow over and bloom, move to the next ruin.
**Feeling:** quiet wonder, hope, the pride of having made something beautiful. Solarpunk: nature and people healing a place together.
**Atoms:** restoration payoff, place-and-grow, regrowth look, no fail state.

### Chicory
**Heart:** a world takes on shape and color that you put there.
**Loop:** paint, watch a gray place bloom, discover what your color unlocks, paint the next place.
**Feeling:** creative ownership, cozy discovery, the world visibly changed by you.
**Atoms:** paint the world, color bloom, restoration payoff, creative ownership.

### Fez
**Heart:** perspective is the mechanic: you look at a small world from every angle, and the answers are in how you look. Cryptic puzzles are great, but they must stay fair.
**Loop:** rotate the view, notice what lines up, solve, uncover a deeper layer of secrets.
**Feeling:** wonder, the click of an insight, being rewarded for paying attention. Too obfuscated and it turns into guessing, which he does not like.
**Atoms:** rotatable world, perspective secret, cryptic-but-fair clues.

### DuckTales (the pogo cane)
**Heart:** one unique, satisfying traversal move carries the whole game.
**Loop:** bounce, chain bounces over hazards, find new places the bounce can reach.
**Feeling:** bouncy mastery, joy in a single move that feels right.
**Atoms:** one traversal verb, bounce traverse, toy-like core verb.

### Katamari Damacy
**Heart:** unabashedly kooky and silly; growing by rolling things up, where each new size opens a new world of targets.
**Loop:** roll up small things, grow, roll up bigger things, repeat until the scale is absurd.
**Feeling:** silliness, escalating delight, growth you can see and feel.
**Atoms:** roll and grow, scale-up loop, kooky tone.

### Lego Party and WarioWare
**Heart:** bite-sized things that are not complex and never overstay their welcome; the next one arrives before the last one gets old.
**Loop:** a short, single-idea challenge, a quick result, the next one.
**Feeling:** light, quick, funny; momentum from variety, no grind.
**Atoms:** bite-sized bursts, single-idea challenges, kooky tone.

### VVVVVV
**Heart:** a single, interesting traversal mechanic. The input never changes, the hazards do; learn them one at a time, then meet them layered together in increasingly clever ways.
**Loop:** meet one new hazard alone, learn it, then see it combined with the ones you know.
**Feeling:** quiet teaching, growing mastery, the joy of a combination you now understand.
**Atoms:** one traversal verb, flip the rule, learn-then-layer, silent teaching.

### The Last of Us
**Heart:** one resource with two valid uses (offense or defense) so every spend is a painful sacrifice of the other.
**Loop:** scavenge, decide what to spend it on, feel what the other choice would have saved.
**Feeling:** weight in every decision, scarcity that matters.
**Atoms:** either-or spend, scarcity, sacrifice choice.

### Townscaper
**Heart:** a sandbox toy; simple placement produces layouts you did not plan, and discovering a new one is the delight.
**Loop:** place, watch the structure reshape itself, wonder what else it can make, place again.
**Feeling:** surprise and delight, calm, play for its own sake.
**Atoms:** sandbox surprise, place-and-grow, no fail state.

### Hot Wheels Unleashed
**Heart:** always on the verge of either catastrophe or triumph. The boost is unwieldy, chaotic and dangerous; when you get it just right you rocket past everyone.
**Loop:** race on the edge, trigger a risky boost, survive or wreck, go again.
**Feeling:** thrill, risk and reward in the same moment, a chaotic power that has to be earned.
**Atoms:** edge of catastrophe, boost gamble, chaotic power, toy-like traversal.

## Negative cards
- **Smash Bros. / Counter-Strike:** mastery through repeated humiliation and technical skill walls. Do not make this the main loop.
- **Fortnite / online multiplayer:** not wanted. No online, no PvP.
- **Minecraft:** weak signal (watched, never played). Slone prefers sandboxes with back pressure over slack ones.
- **Gritty theming:** drop it. Keep the loop, change the skin.

## Cross-card patterns
1. Back pressure on open-ended play.
2. Soft failure that generates stories.
3. Discovery of new play patterns (verbs, items, vehicles).
4. Single-player, self-paced.
5. Verb first, theme second.
6. Readable systems that accumulate consequences.
7. Dense small worlds that open up with new powers.
8. **Transformation as the payoff.** Seeing something dead, gray or empty come alive in front of you can carry a game with little or no pressure (Cloud Gardens, Chicory, Townscaper). This sits beside pattern 1: either something pushes back, or something pulls (a world waiting to be revealed). Open-ended play needs one of the two; the best has both.
9. **One verb, many questions.** A single distinctive move, and the game keeps asking it new things one at a time, then layered (VVVVVV, DuckTales).
10. **Short and sharp.** Mechanics and challenges end before they overstay (WarioWare, Lego Party).
11. **Costly choices.** One resource with two good uses, so every spend hurts (The Last of Us); a boost or power that is dangerous to use (Hot Wheels Unleashed).
12. **Cryptic, not obfuscated.** Mystery is welcome when every secret has a findable clue (Fez).
13. **Kooky is welcome.** Silliness and strange scale are good (Katamari, Lego Party).
