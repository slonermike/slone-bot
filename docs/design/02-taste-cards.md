# 02 Taste Cards (read-only; maintained by Slone)

Each card strips a game he loves to its heart: the loop and the feeling, not the theme or the surface mechanics. Use these to understand what to aim for, never to clone.

## Format
**Heart:** one line. **Loop:** the repeating cycle. **Feeling:** what it does to the player. **Atoms:** concept-library tags.

## Loves

### Tony Hawk
**Heart:** quick runs, open-ended traversal mechanics, repeat until you perfect the line. It is not about skateboarding.
**Loop:** short timed run, chain verbs into a combo, chase a better line, restart instantly.
**Feeling:** flow, mastery by self-chosen repetition (not humiliation), "one more run".
**Atoms:** short-run loop, combo chain, traversal toy, instant restart, self-set goals.

### The Legend of Zelda (all, especially Ocarina of Time; not Tears of the Kingdom)
**Heart:** a dense, learnable world where every new tool recolors places you have already been.
**Loop:** find an item, gain a verb, revisit the world with new eyes, find the next thing.
**Feeling:** curiosity, competence growing steadily, readable combat (targeting).
**Atoms:** item-as-new-verb, revisit-with-power, dense small world, readable lock-on combat.

### RollerCoaster Tycoon
**Heart:** build a system, then watch tiny people react to it. Tweak, observe, ride your creation.
**Loop:** place, observe consequences, adjust, grow.
**Feeling:** quiet pride, tinkering, delightful small stories from simulated people.
**Atoms:** build-and-watch, simulated crowd, readable system, consequence accumulation.

### Tetris
**Heart:** constant pressure with always-readable choices; perfect fits feel like relief.
**Loop:** piece arrives, decide, place, clear, speed ramps until you break.
**Feeling:** tension and release, clean decisions, flow.
**Atoms:** escalating pressure, readable choices, tension-release, speed ramp.

### Red Dead Redemption
**Heart:** weighty movement through a reactive world where travel itself produces unplanned encounters.
**Loop:** ride, stumble into something, handle it, ride on.
**Feeling:** immersion, unplanned stories, being a person in a place.
**Atoms:** weighty traversal, emergent encounters, reactive world.

### Mario
**Heart:** the movement is the toy; it feels good before any level exists; level design teaches silently.
**Loop:** move, discover what movement can do, apply it to a new layout.
**Feeling:** playfulness, quiet teaching, joy in motion.
**Atoms:** toy-like core verb, silent teaching, movement mastery by exploration.

### Metroid
**Heart:** isolation, plus ability gating that makes you revisit old spaces more powerful.
**Loop:** explore, hit a wall, find a power, return, open the wall.
**Feeling:** solitude, discovery, growing power.
**Atoms:** ability gating, revisit-with-power, isolation atmosphere.

### Timberborn
**Heart:** logistics against a looming constraint (drought, tides); you live inside your layout's consequences.
**Loop:** plan, build, survive the pressure cycle, learn from the damage, redesign.
**Feeling:** tense stewardship, satisfying engineering, back pressure that gives open-ended play stakes.
**Atoms:** looming cyclical pressure, logistics layout, consequence of design, recoverable setback.

### The Sims
**Heart:** a household with a history; steady growth, successes, and recovering from failures.
**Loop:** tend needs, make choices, watch outcomes accumulate, recover from setbacks.
**Feeling:** long-term attachment, narrative accumulation, comfort in recovery.
**Atoms:** household with a history, recoverable setback loop, narrative accumulation.

### GTA / Saints Row (minus the grit)
**Heart:** a toy-box city where driving is the base verb. Heat and chases create pressure, and constant discovery of new vehicles, weapons, and play patterns keeps exploration paying out.
**Loop:** roam, find a new toy, cause a little chaos, escape the heat, repeat.
**Feeling:** thrilling escape, curiosity about what else is out there, playful freedom.
**Atoms:** escalating pursuit, toy-like traversal (driving), new-verb discovery, open sandbox with back pressure.

## Negative cards
- **Smash Bros. / Counter-Strike:** mastery through repeated humiliation and technical skill walls. Do not make this the main loop.
- **Fortnite / online multiplayer:** not wanted. No online, no PvP.
- **Minecraft:** weak signal (watched, never played). Slone prefers sandboxes with back pressure over slack ones.
- **Gritty theming:** drop it. Keep the loop, change the skin.

## Cross-card patterns
1. Back pressure on open-ended play.
2. Soft failure that generates stories.
3. Discovery of new play patterns (verbs, items, vehicles).
4. Single-player, self-paced.
5. Verb first, theme second.
6. Readable systems that accumulate consequences.
7. Dense small worlds that open up with new powers.
