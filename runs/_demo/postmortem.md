# Post mortem: Demo Ball
This is the scaffold's smoke-test prototype, not a nightly build. It exists to prove input, sim, render, audio, telemetry, bots, manifest, and launcher work end to end, and it has been revised once from Slone's first play.
What shipped: push a ball with the stick, dash with a button, collect sparks. Pressure comes in layers over a run (heat timer, then drifters at 25s, fading sparks at 55s, closing walls at 90s). Three failures end the run, with a result screen and instant restart. Each failure restarts the heat ramp.
Forward pressure: combos fill a surge meter; when full a prompt appears and the player chooses when to spend it. Surge: about 5 seconds at constant speed, steer only, heat frozen, every drifter touched dies spectacularly (more drifters spawn so there is something to smash).
Balance: eight knobs in the pause menu's Balance screen (heat ramp, heat relief, drifter hit heat, surge meter speed, surge length, drifter speed, layer pace, lives); values save per device and can be copied into chat.
Measured (bots, sim only): a greedy bot earns a surge every 30 to 50 seconds, reaches all four layers, and loses its last life at about 3.5 to 4 minutes; idle, random, and corner-camping bots end within about 40 to 50 seconds.
Believed (labeled, I cannot play or hear it): the failure crunch and the layer stings should read clearly; the pacing of the layers is a guess to check against your play.
