# Brief: Blot Beat   (2026-10-30-blotbeat)
Question: Does a rhythm game where every miss becomes a resource (ink you can spend) make mistakes feel like fuel instead of failure, while keeping a real tension about how much ink to carry?
Target feeling: cheeky, flowing "controlled mess": the relief of a big stamp after nearly overflowing; rhythmic flow, never humiliation.
Mode: content-driven (4 short sets, about 4 minutes), with a real run end (3 pages) and a win.
Theme: "Every mistake leaves a mark that later becomes a tool"   Why picked: the nightly roll was already consumed by the previous night on the same date and the guard refuses a second roll; I wrote this prompt myself (logged in run health). It is a mechanic: failure feeds forward power.
Genre: rhythm action   Options considered: twin-stick arena survivor, physics puzzle, garden/tending sim, rhythm action, side-view platformer (rolled: rhythm action)
Atoms: f-juice-forward, l-roguelite-run      Wildcard: none
Look (rolled, no override): mono-plus-one (plum in five tints + white + lime accent), thin lines (wireframes, mostly empty space), thick outlines (fat dark ink edge under everything), faux isometric (tabletop diamond board), stickers UI (badges). Colors: #120a22 ink outline, #2e1b52 bg, #4a2f86 board, #7857c4 lines, #b9a3ee light tint, #ffffff, accent #c6ff3d lime = the mark of a mistake and the stamp. At rest: a dark plum desk with a pale wireframe diamond board, four thin lanes running into a ring, a little inkwell with eyes in the middle.
Loop (4 lines):
 - win/reward: play four sets of a song; score and combo fill each page's "signature" bar; a finished run is signed. Stickers picked between sets change the rules.
 - obstacle/pressure: notes come in on four lanes; a missed note or a stray mash drops an ink blot on the board. Blots fill the ink well. New note kinds arrive set by set (holds, doubles, a fast finale).
 - fail state and recovery: ink overflow spills the page (lose 1 of 3 pages), the board clears, a short rest, ink resets. Out of pages ends the run. A spill is a reprieve, not a wall.
 - actions: strike a lane (Y north, B east, A south, X west, or flick the stick), hold long notes, spend 3 blots on a STAMP (RB) that auto-plays the next notes and starts a fever (double score), pick a sticker between sets.
Core verb: strike a lane on the beat. It is a toy first: every lane is a pitch in the key, so tapping plays a little melody and the inkwell squishes and splashes.
Pressure source: ink from misses; set by set the chart gets denser, adds holds, doubles, then everything faster. Pressure gains character, not just speed.
Recovery loop: a miss feeds the stamp; a stamp is a power moment you choose when to spend (hold ink for a bigger fever, or cash in before a spill). Stories: "I was one blot from spilling, stamped, and sailed through the doubles."
Different from the last five nights (from the ledger only): the other nights were an arcade survival and an ice tower defense; this is a real-time rhythm game, plum wireframe iso board, where the failure resource is also the power resource.
Scope cut line: in: 4 lanes, tap/hold/double notes, ink and stamp, 3 pages, 6 stickers, between-set pick, win and loss screens, own music with parts that follow ink. Out: song select, per-device latency calibration, mines, meta unlocks across runs.
Feel plan: hit = pitch per lane + sparkle on perfect, ink splash particles, squash on the inkwell, ring flash, number pop; miss = lime blot lands on the board with a splat sound and shake scaled to ink level; ink high = music thins, a nervous beat, wobbling well; stamp = big hit-stop, shake, lime ring wave, stinger; spill = layered crash, hold beat, page tears.
Screens and UI plan: HUD as stickers/badges: top-left pages (sheet icons), top-center set badge with song progress rail, top-right score plate with combo multiplier badge; bottom-center ink well made of drop slots right beside the "RB STAMP" key-cap chip (currency next to where it is spent); unlike meters apart and different in shape (drops vs rail vs sheets). Pick screen: three horizontal sticker cards with LB/RB chips at the ends and A to confirm. Win: "SIGNED!" sequence then tally counting up then cards. Loss: plain reason ("Out of pages: too much ink"), tally, cards.
Balance knobs: window (hit window scale, 1.0, 0.6-1.6), inkMax (blots before a spill, 8, 4-14), stampCost (blots to stamp, 3, 1-6), feverMult (score multiplier in fever, 2, 1-4), pages (lives, 3, 1-6), speed (tempo scale, 1.0, 0.8-1.25).
Telemetry plan: score, hits/perfects/misses, blots, stamps, spills, pages lost, setReached, combo max. Bots: greedy (perfect play with a small error and sensible stamping), degenerate-mash (hits every lane every beat), plus builtins.
Feedback questions:
 1. Did a miss ever feel like fuel rather than punishment? when?
 2. Did you hold ink on purpose to stamp bigger, or cash in right away?
 3. Could you tell what each note kind wanted (tap, hold, double) the first time you saw it?
 4. Did the sound of your hits feel like playing the music, or like noise on top of it?
 5. Which sticker did you pick first and did it change how you played?
