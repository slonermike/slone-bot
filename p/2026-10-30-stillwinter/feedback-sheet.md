THE QUESTION THIS BUILD TESTS (written before you played):
Does tower defense feel fresh when the world is frozen and the only way to move time is to break your own defenses?
TARGET FEELING: tense, crafty stewardship: a held breath, then a thrilling release

TARGETED QUESTIONS:
1. The first time you shattered a spire on purpose, how did it feel? satisfying / meh / confusing
2. Did "time only moves when something breaks" change how you planned, compared to other tower defense games? How?
3. Did you ever stall (stay frozen) because you were afraid to let time loose? When?
4. Did Cold Snap feel earned and big enough? (How many times did you use it?)
5. Did you find anything I did not tell you about? (fault lines? warm fronts? chains?)
6. Could you tell what each tower does just from how it looks and moves? Which one was unclear?
7. Did the dawn sequence (sun, beacon powers up, enemies broken in turn) read clearly as a win? Did "Endless Night" make sense?
