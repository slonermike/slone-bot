# Post mortem: Blot Beat (2026-10-30-blotbeat)
Question and target feeling: does a rhythm game where every miss becomes spendable ink make mistakes feel like fuel while keeping real tension about how much ink to carry? Cheeky, flowing controlled mess: the relief of a big stamp after nearly overflowing.
What shipped: a four-lane rhythm game on an isometric ink-desk (Y/B/A/X or stick flicks, taps then holds then doubles over four sets). A miss drops a blot that flies into the inkwell; RB stamps all of it into a fever (x2 score, misses cost half, the board is auto-hit), too much ink spills a page (3 pages, a real run end), from set 3 a half-full well leaks, clean sets win a page back, and a sticker is picked between sets.
Rounds: 4 rounds and 1 step-back before wrap. R1 core economy (stamp spam made fever a free pass, spill cleared the whole chart: both fixed). R2 look and readability (taller board, double-note shape, timing offset knob, tests). Step-back: pressure was only "misses", so holding ink cost nothing, which led to the leak, lane streaks, beat ring, pictogram stickers and the win power-up. R3 found a tween bug that hid every prompt plate, and a headless fps problem. R4 closed the mash loophole (a stray now costs a blot), lengthened the sets and added count-in numbers, then Tidy Set.
Core before polish: the bare economy was run with sim-only bots before any drawing existed; it was not run with juice off in a browser (untested). Judged by bot spread: idle fails at 29 s, random 32 s, stamp-only 79 s, mash 72 s, sloppy (79% accurate) finishes at 52k, greedy (92%) at 96k.
Did it work (honest read): feel checklist by my count 36 pass, 1 fail (no trails on notes), 4 n/a (hit-stop on purpose, camera, coyote/buffer equivalents), 15 untested (every [Slone] item and real-hardware fps). Shakiest: music-to-note sync, difficulty, and whether the sounds read as playing the song.
Measured (telemetry): five seeds each. Greedy 92% accuracy, 31 misses, 5 stamps, 0 spills, max combo 55, 0.8 tidy sets, 4.2 drips. Sloppy 79%, 102 misses, 19 stamps, 0 spills. Novice 70%, 143 misses, 18 stamps, 0.8 spills. Input to audio 14 ms. Frame rate: 8 fps in headless software GL (the trivial fixture runs 59 there); one full-screen layer costs about 90 ms in that renderer. Real-hardware fps is NOT measured.
Believed (labeled): the stamp should feel good, the leak should make "hold or cash in" a real choice, the offset knob will be needed. I cannot play or hear the build, so none of this is verified.
Degenerate strategies found: stamp-only farming (fixed by a stamp lock and half ink in fever), mashing all lanes (fixed: strays cost a blot and break the combo). A sticker-less greedy run still never spills, so the economy is easy for precise play.
What I cut or ran out of time for: song select, latency calibration screen, mines or other note kinds, cross-run unlocks, trails on notes, juice-off browser run, more than three picks.
Surprises: see runs/2026-10-30-blotbeat/surprises.md. Biggest: the real game appeared only once the leak existed (carry more ink for a longer fever, or cash in). No pivot was needed.
Biggest risk for Slone to check: timing feel. If hits feel early or late against the music, change Timing offset first (EARLY/LATE tags show on non-perfect hits).
Reusable tech proposed to main: none code. Doc note proposed: the juice tween manager replaces a tween on the same property, so a delayed second tween on one property cancels the first (use a dummy-target delay).
Assets sourced: all synthesized (sounds and music generated in code, no third-party assets).
Theme picked and why: self-authored "Every mistake leaves a mark that later becomes a tool" because the night's roll was already used by the previous night on the same date and the script refused a second one (the roll guard is per calendar date).
Themes added: none. The script refused (the 3-per-date cap was already used by the earlier night dated 2026-10-30). Candidates for Slone to add: "The thing you spend is also the measure of how badly you are doing" (system); "A store that only leaks while you are holding too much of it" (system).
Next time (one data point): when failure produces the player's power resource, add a relief-valve cost and a second pressure that punishes hoarding, or the optimal play is to cash in on reflex.
